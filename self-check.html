BEFORE YOU BEGIN

How do you study now?

Six questions about what you actually do, not what you intend to do. Answer honestly, especially where you suspect the habit is weak. Nothing you enter here is saved, scored, or sent anywhere. The result is for you.

PRIVATE SELF-CHECK

Six questions

Pick the answer closest to your real habit over the last few weeks.

QUESTION 1 OF 6You finish reading a section of a chapter. What do you usually do next?
QUESTION 2 OF 6You answer a practice question and get it right. What do you do with the rationale?
QUESTION 3 OF 6How do you decide a topic is studied enough?
QUESTION 4 OF 6When do you usually do most of your studying for an exam?
QUESTION 5 OF 6You get a practice question wrong. What happens next?
QUESTION 6 OF 6Think of a topic you studied last week. Could you explain it out loud right now, without notes?
